PROJECT / 01

Mobile Robot Auto-Aiming System

Vision Algorithm Team Lead, 2024 Season · USTB Reborn Robotics Team

Adapted an open-source auto-aiming framework to our team’s robots through parameter tuning and deployment. Worked with teammates to replace armor detection with a YOLOv8-Pose four-keypoint model and improve aiming logic, integrating vision, state estimation, and aiming decisions on real hardware.

C++ / PythonROS 2 HumbleYOLOv8-PoseOpenVINOOpenCV
Detection model repository↗
Technical pipeline & historical tests
Keypoint detection→PnP pose estimation→EKF state estimation→Aiming decisions

Deployed the vision model on an Intel NUC with OpenVINO and connected detection, tracking, and gimbal interfaces through ROS 2. Aiming angles account for predicted target motion, armor selection, and ballistic compensation.

Historical hardware test record: over 80% hit accuracy and under 12 ms of end-to-end algorithm processing within 10 m, under test conditions of 2 rad/s target rotation and 1 m/s translation.

These figures come from a historical resume; complete records of the test platform, sample size, and timing boundaries are unavailable. The system builds on Chen Jun’s open-source rm_vision / rm_auto_aim framework. My contribution focused on team-specific adaptation, deployment, collaborative detection improvements, and aiming logic.
